01 Music rights platform · 2025 — Now A royalty platform that hunts for the money you didn't get paid. Software engineer
Dotted Eighth LLC

Most royalty tools report what landed. This platform checks what should have been paid: statements, cue sheets and catalogue data are cross-examined, and every finding arrives with its evidence and a person to confirm it.

The problem

Publishers are paid from statements produced by dozens of sources. Missing registrations, splits and placements show up as silence, not errors.

What I built

Full-stack product work across the React front end, Express/Prisma API and PostgreSQL data layer: statement ingestion and income tracking, cue-sheet checks, catalogue metadata, matching, valuation, permissions and white-label instances.

What changed

Dedicated instances now run real client catalogues, and the import pipeline went from about 68 minutes to 12. Findings surface as a review queue with evidence — never as an automatic claim.

02 Music AI Suite · 2025 — Now An AI layer over a 55,000-work classical catalogue. Software engineer
Dotted Eighth LLC

Catalogue chat, sheet-music analysis and a data-quality butler: AI that reads the catalogue and flags what humans should look at, never edits it on its own.

The problem

A classical publisher's catalogue spans tens of thousands of works, hundreds of thousands of performances and decades of inconsistent metadata.

What I built

An embeddable React chat with SSE streaming over catalogue data, a sheet-music scanner that analyses and compares PDFs page by page with live progress, and an AI butler that runs duplicate, missing-field and conflict rules into a human review queue.

What changed

Enrichment now runs across 15+ music and rights sources with entity resolution and proxy fallbacks. The chat ships both inside a publisher site and as a client-side widget.

03 AI productivity suite · 2024 — 2026 Text and image AI, without the model wrangling. Software engineer
Stealth startup

A productivity platform that hides LLMs and diffusion models behind everyday tools: writing, image editing and assisted workflows for people who don't want a prompt engineering degree.

The problem

Powerful models are useless to non-technical users when every task starts with a blank prompt box and ends with a broken output.

What I built

Modular Node/Express services for tweet generation, essay composition and image manipulation, a Next.js/React front end, and an abstraction layer that keeps model choice out of the user's way.

What changed

Production-ready flows for everyday users, with the model complexity handled server-side and the interface reduced to the task at hand.

04 Aptask JobTalk · 2023 — 2024 An AI recruiter that cut screening time by 70%. Software developer
Aptask

An end-to-end voice agent for candidate screening: multi-turn conversations, intent recognition, entity extraction and a live dashboard for the humans running the hire.

The problem

Recruiters spend their days on repetitive first-round calls, and the useful signal — intent, experience, availability — is buried in notes.

What I built

LLM conversation flows with dynamic intent and entity extraction, a React/TypeScript front end, Node/Express services, CSV matching pipelines, WebSocket call monitoring and a 15+ KPI analytics dashboard.

What changed

Screening time dropped by 70%, while recruiters kept real-time visibility into every call instead of waiting for a transcript.
